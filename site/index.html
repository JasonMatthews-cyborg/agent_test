Method & limits

Scoring scale

Each score also shows its basis (a record of actions, statements only, or a mix) and a confidence level. A statement shows what a candidate intends. It does not show that the proposal would work.

Rules applied

  • Positions are never inferred from party alone.
  • No prior federal office is not counted as poor performance. Chew's execution score reflects thin evidence, not a penalty.
  • Policies that add supply are separated from those that subsidize demand (e.g., a mortgage-rate subsidy counts against building abundance).
  • Energy is judged by likely effect on supply, not by which technologies a candidate likes.
  • Research support and deployment support are scored separately within technology.
  • Contrary evidence was sought for each candidate and is shown in red in the evidence lists.
